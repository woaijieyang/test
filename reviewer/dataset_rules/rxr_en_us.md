# Reviewer guidance — RxR en-US

Review the complete original instruction and actual frozen candidate independently. Use the unchanged common checklist, L0–L6 order, dimensions, evidence rules and active schema. Do not treat the Generator's audit, a paired example's verdict, or a navigation score as proof.

## Audit order within the existing layers

L0: Check the actual rendered artifact and candidate correspondence. Renderer whitespace is not, by itself, a semantic defect.

L1: Map meaningful source events, identifiers, counts, conditions, constraints and final placement to indexed candidate evidence. Mark an explicitly corrected source clause as superseded only within its supported scope. A repeated clause may describe the same active event; do not require another traversal solely to obtain a separate coverage row.

L2: Check event identity, correction scope, temporal trigger ownership, referents and spatial relation ownership. Determine the subject, reference frame and time of each left/right/between/facing relation. Distinguish real conditional action from conditional observation and descriptive uncertainty. Inspect count origins and genuine return history across every cut.

L3: Independently check Entry, Target, Process, Ending and Conditions in the actual English. An immediately established unambiguous state can be inherited without a new From/After sentence. Conversely, an invented previous arrival cannot support the next stage. Keep final bounded movement, entry depth and facing constraints with their actual event where appropriate.

L4: Compare every preceding exit with the next first unperformed action. Cite both sides for cross-stage faults. Require explicit anchors only where a necessary count, frame, identity or persistent constraint would otherwise be lost. Do not demand redundant narration or a physical stop at every boundary.

L5: Check both buried independent objectives and cuts that detach a coupled maneuver or endpoint refinement. A short final stage is neither automatically wrong nor automatically useful. A different valid segmentation is not a defect. Under-segmentation needs an earlier completion, distinct later objective, supported handoff and concrete local-focus loss.

L6: Perform reverse support as well as coverage. Locate the source authority for every newly explicit turn, visit, repeat, arrival, certainty claim and maintained constraint. Check the final location, relative position and facing together. Re-read the entire candidate, not only the changed fragment.

## Findings and false-positive controls

A blocking finding requires an actual material defect, exact source evidence, indexed candidate evidence, affected stages and a minimal source-faithful repair. Explain the particular execution consequence. Do not block on grammar, style, synonymous wording, an alternative valid segmentation, or navigation failure alone. Absence of images is not source ambiguity. Do not use audit prose to compensate for missing candidate meaning.

For repetition, require evidence of an additional action before alleging a missing repeat; also catch unsupported `again`. For corrections, identify what was superseded and protect everything outside that scope. For conditions, do not apply a blanket if/or keyword rule. For spatial phrases, do not replace an ambiguous source with the reviewer's preferred layout.

## Gate and recheck

Keep the existing PASS/REVISE/UNRESOLVED semantics. PASS means the candidate satisfies the language contract, not that navigation will succeed. REVISE requires a repairable candidate defect. UNRESOLVED is reserved for remaining route-critical source ambiguity. The Generator remains the only candidate author. During recheck, independently inspect the entire new candidate, disposition every previous finding using new candidate evidence, and report new material defects. Do not approve merely because the Generator says a repair was made.
