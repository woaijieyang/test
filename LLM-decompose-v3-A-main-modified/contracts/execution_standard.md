# Shared execution standard — v1.3-static.2

This is the SAME semantic standard for generator and reviewer. Route data comes only from original_instruction. Adapter/renderer metadata describe bytes and provenance, not world state. None of these checks establishes that an action has happened at runtime.

## Priority and the two groups retained from v1.3
Priority: preserve meaning and dependencies; ensure completion and handoff; obtain useful local focus; minimize rewriting.

Five stage elements: supported entry context; full prescribed process; target and identification; completion event/state; applicable conditions/constraints. They are meanings, NOT five mandatory phrases or five cut points. A named object alone is not proof of completion. A first-stage starting context may be the actual unspecified episode start. Later entry may be naturally established by its action context and a source-supported preceding state; use a brief explicit anchor where identity, count or viewpoint would otherwise be ambiguous.

Five relation families: until; after/once/when; before; while/keep/stay; if/otherwise/either/or. Bind each to its event and scope. A cut must preserve entity identity, counting origin, orientation reference and still-active constraints. If a phase ends at a doorway, do not silently begin the next phase inside it. A planned after-state is not an observed completed state.

Evidence levels: S1 is explicitly stated in the source. S2 restores a relationship uniquely supported by the whole source, such as a later turn-at-X trigger closing the preceding movement or a same-event halfway refinement. S3 is an unambiguous local language repair that does not choose a new route interpretation. S4 is scene convention, imagination, GT or another episode and is forbidden as route evidence. S3 alone cannot supply a missing route endpoint.

## C01–C12 review checklist
C01 Active action/route coverage, including distinct repeats and returns.
C02 No unsupported additions or strengthening: stop, enter, reach, attributes, quantities.
C03 Event order and identity: refinement, correction and new event are different.
C04 Trigger/termination/continuous/branch ownership and AND/OR/NOT scope.
C05 Identifying cues are attached before the target must be selected.
C06 Spatial relation owner, reference entity, viewpoint and time are preserved.
C07 Coreference, selected entity and counting origin persist across cuts.
C08 Entry/process/target/constraints/ending exist with source-supported completion evidence.
C09 Previous exit supports next necessary entry; preserve history and frame, not just a location name.
C10 Useful segmentation: neither dependency-breaking fragmentation nor clearly missed independent phases.
C11 Intermediate stop scope and final position plus orientation are preserved.
C12 The actual English is executable language, without editorial notes or a missing requirement supplied only in an audit summary.

## Three independent verdict dimensions
Fidelity: is the prescribed route preserved in both directions?
Closure/handoff: does each phase have its complete process and supported ending, and do adjacent states connect?
Segmentation adequacy: has the candidate usefully decomposed genuinely distinct local objectives without needless fragmentation?

A source-exact whole route can be faithful yet undersegmented. A concrete missed phase may justify revision as a TASK-QUALITY defect, not a claimed meaning change. Such a finding must show the actual feasible boundary, distinct next objective and supported handoff. Merely preferring three lines to two is not material. A coherent single-objective instruction can correctly remain one phase. Single phase does NOT mean no semantic assembly is allowed. Do not optimize a fixed stage count, word limit, KEEP rate or reject rate.

## Limits
Normal visual grounding, absent coordinates and unspecified numerical tolerances are not source defects. Check all clauses for a supported ending before flagging a gap. Explicit arrival/traversal/stop, an intrinsically bounded action, or a uniquely supported later trigger/refinement can establish completion. If the original genuinely supplies none, do not invent arrival or STOP. Source-level uncertainty and a candidate-induced defect must be reported separately. Textual pass is not a navigation-success or non-regression certificate.

## Applying one standard consistently
Semantic closure is not a five-phrase template. A process and its necessary endpoint/refinement remain together, and a supported transition may be a boundary only when it has useful phase focus. Assess all three dimensions, but do not create a quota of cuts, findings or passes. Single-task assembly, exact-source acceptance, source-limited fallback and technical fallback are distinct outcomes. A location-bound wait/stand can specify a target placement without a separately written walk command.

The reviewer must plan which checks matter for this source, then provide concise verifiable evidence. Report one root defect once, listing related checks/dimensions rather than repeating it. Feedback is evidence-based advice. Only the generator edits candidate language; adapter/renderer may restore verified source whitespace before freezing, never invent route content. No change after review is permitted.
