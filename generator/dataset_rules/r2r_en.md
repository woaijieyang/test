# R2R Generator: preserve, organize, express

Use only for `r2r-en`. P2 applies the named P1 mechanism; every P1 inherits G-P0 and the shared R2R meanings S-ROLE/S-SCOPE/S-BOUNDARY/S-ENTRY/S-REPAIR. Recheck the parent requirements after each edit. Return the existing initial or repair Schema.

## G-P0 — Preserve the complete requested route

Read the whole source and retain its actions, target identities, constraints, uncertainty and final placement. Organize it into executable local objectives with the least necessary textual change. Resolve a candidate-created gap through supported binding, boundary movement or merging. Use the source-limited path only when an essential route choice remains genuinely unresolved.

## G-P1-A — Build the event and relation structure

Under G-P0 and S-ROLE/S-SCOPE, identify what happens, to which entity, in what order, under which condition, and where the route ultimately ends. Read later clauses before finalizing earlier movements.

### G-P2-A1 — Attributes, observations and source corrections

Assign each phrase its operative role. Keep an already-open door as an identifying property and a predicted view as an orientation cue. Bind a later descriptor to the entity it identifies before that entity is selected. Resolve grammar from the complete context; require unique source support for a changed number, direction or destination. Preserve uncertain route-defining wording when that support is absent.

### G-P2-A2 — Relation owners and counting origins

Carry the reference entity and active frame with each spatial relation. Preserve a room's position relative to the staircase even after reaching the staircase. Keep path-wide relations such as on/along/with on your side over their stated movement. Retain the count's object class, start and region when selecting an ordinal door, tile, step or flight.

### G-P2-A3 — Refinement, branches and repeated events

Bind until/when/halfway to the movement they limit. Retain source-prescribed motion between separate stops. Keep an explicitly intermediate STOP before the continuing route and the final WAIT at its own location. Preserve if/otherwise as alternatives. For a return, keep the same entity identity and both the outward and return events. Check the terminal scope again whenever a clause is relocated.

## G-P1-B — Group complete maneuvers and supported handoffs

Under G-P0 and S-BOUNDARY/S-ENTRY, assemble coupled actions before selecting cuts. Evaluate the actual prior completion, next objective, usable entry and focus benefit together. Let the event structure determine the number of stages.

### G-P2-B1 — Transit and a new local objective

Keep a room exit with the turn and traversal that it prepares when the room is transit context. Split where a completed region transition leads to a genuinely different source-supported target or selection. Keep pass-by references attached to their movement; use a separately stated visit only when the source requests one.

### G-P2-B2 — Inheritance and anchors

Begin the next stage with its first unperformed action. Inherit uniquely established pose or entity. Add only the phrase needed to bind an otherwise unclear doorway, region, count origin or frame. For a hallway-entry requirement, express the entry before using the hallway as the next stage's starting region. Preserve unresolved modifier attachment instead of creating a completed intermediate landmark to justify a cut.

## G-P1-C — Write and verify the actual instruction

Under G-P0 and S-REPAIR, give each edit an execution-relevant purpose. Keep unaffected wording. Supply the existing five meanings and handoffs as grounded self-audit; executable requirements belong in the stage text itself.

### G-P2-C1 — Exact endings and economical English

Preserve before/near/inside/outside/between and final facing at their actual endpoint. Keep a source-directed final approach as an approach. Express a terminal refinement with the action it completes; preserve a separate placement stage when it genuinely adds a supported local objective. Then read source-to-candidate and candidate-to-source for missing or added requirements.

### G-P2-C2 — Independent repair

For each indexed Reviewer suggestion, accept its supported part, preserve every unaffected requirement and reject any unsupported addition with exact source evidence. A correct diagnosis can have an incorrect proposed repair. Recheck the entire route after the local change, especially quantity, direction, target identity and terminal scope. An unchanged valid candidate is a legitimate response to unsupported advice.
