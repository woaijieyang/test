# R2R interpretation boundaries

Apply only to `r2r-en`, under the shared semantic contract. The role guides below implement these meanings; their P2 operations inherit P1 and P0. Priority describes dependency, not error severity. Read the full source before using a case-specific rule.

## S-ROLE — Preserve the role of each phrase

Identify the commanded action, its target, identifying attributes, path references, and expected observations separately. An open door can identify a passage; a facing clause can establish a reference. Preserve each role when reassembling English. Use the whole source to resolve references and non-route-changing grammar; retain genuine uncertainty where competing readings select different routes.

## S-SCOPE — Keep each relation with its owner

Track the entity being related, its reference, the affected movement and its time span. Keep `on` over the prescribed path, `from` at the origin, object-relative left/right with that object, and ordinal class/origin across stages. Read later clauses before fixing an endpoint: bind a refinement to the same movement while retaining every intervening action and the original intermediate/terminal stop scope. Interpret mutually exclusive branches as alternatives and revisits as repeated events involving the same entity.

## S-BOUNDARY — Complete a maneuver before judging a cut

Assemble dependent turn/move, approach/trigger, transit-through-room and target/description relations first. A useful cut has a supported prior outcome, a distinct next objective, an executable entry and a concrete focus benefit. A completed entry makes a cut eligible; its value depends on the remaining objective. Both compact multi-goal routes and longer single maneuvers are possible. Accept source-supported alternative granularity under C10.

## S-ENTRY — Carry only necessary context

Start the next stage with its first unperformed action. Inherit uniquely established pose or entities; state a short anchor when target identity, count origin, region or reference frame otherwise becomes unclear. Describe the intended outcome as a prerequisite, not an observed success. Keep pass-by, approach, entry and arrival distinct, including the allowed final target-directed approach.

## S-REPAIR — Edit locally and recheck globally

Give each edit a specific source-supported purpose and preserve unaffected requirements. A Reviewer suggestion is a proposal, not new route evidence. Check high-impact changes to quantity, direction, identity, ordering and final placement against the complete source. Use the existing evidence and verdict contract. Templates demonstrate decisions for their own source/candidate; their nouns, stage counts and verdicts are not instructions for the current route.
