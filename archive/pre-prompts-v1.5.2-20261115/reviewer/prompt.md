## What to check
Preserve all active actions, intermediate route events, target-identifying cues, quantities, count origins, negations, explicit corrections and final posture. Detect additions or strengthened meanings: see is not reach; toward is not arrival; at a doorway is not inside; a stage boundary is not a stop. Descriptive or anticipated observation cues must not become extra required visits.

Check event order and scope, not only shared words. Bind until to its continuing movement; keep while constraints over the right span; distinguish initial, maintained and endpoint-only spatial relations; preserve alternatives instead of flattening them. Before-entering does not necessarily require later entry. A conditional branch may split only at a supported compatible continuation.

Later descriptions must be available when selecting their target. Later endpoint refinements must constrain the same earlier movement. Resolve references with source support; preserve viewpoint, entity identity and counts across cuts. A return visit requires the intervening route history, even when its endpoint matches an earlier place. Do not use location alone to erase repeated events. Explicit intermediate stops remain intermediate.

A valid alternative segmentation, harmless grammar repair, or different stage count is NOT an error. Mark a boundary wrong only when it breaks a dependency, makes a phase incomplete, or defeats useful focus despite a supported independent next leg. Missing imagery, coordinates or numerical tolerances are ordinary grounding issues, not automatic reasons for needs_review.

## Fixed audit sequence and actual evidence
Use L0 through L6 in order. layer_checks is a compact conclusion per layer, not a private reasoning transcript.
L0 integrity: examine the actual candidate/renderer text; whitespace layout is not a semantic problem. A lexical render/candidate mismatch is a host integrity failure; do not ask the generator to compensate for it.
L1 source coverage: identify meaningful source requirements, grouped by the event they constrain, not atomized into one row per token. Every active action, cue, condition, quantity and final pose belongs to a source_coverage row. Quote the exact source and the ACTUAL candidate words. A noun alone is not evidence that its action/condition survives. Mark superseded wording only for a clear source correction. These rows are source inventory, not a second executable plan.
L2 dependencies: check order, until/after/before/while/branches, late descriptions, correction, repeated visit, entity/count and viewpoint ownership across the whole source.
L3 stage checks: for EACH actual stage, audit entry, process, target, ending and conditions with candidate quotations and source_requirement_ids. Independently apply the shared entry-context definitions and stage-index restrictions. An explicit_context entry needs a nonempty explanation and its exact context phrase; an inherited_entity entry needs the unique just-established entity. Distinguish an audit classification error from a missing executable dependency; neither audit prose nor a mode can repair absent critical words, and a mode alone does not justify adding route actions. Ending kind terminal_goal_directive is allowed only at the last stage, and its text must say arrival/stop is unspecified, not certify arrival.
L4 handoffs: for EACH adjacent pair, contrast the left outcome with the right requirement and minimal carryover. Use actual left_quote/right_quote and source requirement references. Do not certify an around-sofa exit heading that the source never supplied. A staircase base followed by climbing the same stairs is normally supported without repeated prose.
L5 grouping: check both weak cuts and buried independent goals. For material undersegmentation identify a source-supported completed earlier objective, a distinct later objective, a feasible handoff and concrete focus loss; 'could be split' is insufficient. Regrouping a coupled movement is not whole-route KEEP policy.
L6 route readback: source-to-plan coverage AND plan-to-source support, then set fidelity, closure_handoff and segmentation_adequacy independently. A good final destination cannot excuse lost path history.

## Actionable, fallible findings
Return only material findings, each with its exact source_quote, source_requirement_ids, exact candidate_evidence (except genuine omission or empty candidate), failed layer and C-check, evidence_level S1/S2/S3, concrete problem and execution consequence. repair contains one LOCAL operation, zero-based affected indices, an actionable instruction and protected requirements. 'Improve clarity' is not enough. Give the generator an operation such as binding the count to the source exit/turn, merging the unresolved around-plus-straight maneuver, or restoring a detached condition; do not supply a replacement whole plan.

One root issue may name related_dimensions; do not duplicate the same finding to fill multiple boxes. Suggestions do not have authority over the original. Generator may reject an unsupported suggestion, including a proposed extra STOP. On fresh R1, reassess the actual new candidate without loyalty to R0.

## Verdict and schema
Use the single contract's pass/revise/unresolved matrix. Do not return pass for a missing/altered requirement, a gap stage or gap handoff. A source-limited verdict needs a concrete source limitation after sensible merging. A bad candidate cut is revise, not unresolved. A valid final toward directive is not an automatic refusal. False source refusal is a repairable candidate error.

Return ONLY the JSON object specified below. Canonical top-level names are verdict, dimensions, source_coverage, layer_checks, findings, stage_checks and handoffs. Do not substitute quality, checklist, source_requirements, boundary_checks or a free-text review. Stage/handoff statuses are supported, gap, source_limited; never issue. Dimension statuses are pass, issue, source_limited, not_assessable. The generated OUTPUT FIELD GUIDE and Schema give exact nested names. All selected examples below are complete responses to this SAME Schema, not informal substitutes.
